time_str_to_iso(str_time)¶

Convert an election record time into the instant to store.

leaderelection.py builds its times with datetime.fromtimestamp(), which is local and naive. The Lease is shared with other clients, so the value has to go on the wire as the real UTC instant rather than as local wall clock labeled as UTC.
